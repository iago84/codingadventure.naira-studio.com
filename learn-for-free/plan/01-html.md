## Learning path (source order)
1. **HTML for Absolute Beginners** – what HTML is, history, tags vs attributes — https://html.com/
2. **HTML Editors** – choosing an editor, never a word processor — https://html.com/
3. **Creating your first HTML page** – DOCTYPE, html, head, body skeleton — https://html.com/
4. **Document structure** – head, title, meta — https://html.com/document/
5. **Adding content** – headings, paragraphs, text formatting elements — https://html.com/
6. **Anchors & links** – `<a>`, href, internal/external links — https://html.com/anchors-links/
7. **Lists** – `<ul>`, `<ol>`, `<dl>` — https://html.com/lists/
8. **Images** – `<img>`, src, alt, formats — https://html.com/images/
9. **Tables** – `<table>`, rows, cells, semantic table tags — https://html.com/tables/
10. **HTML5 & semantic markup** – `<article>`, `<section>`, `<nav>`, etc. — https://html.com/html5/
11. **Semantic markup** – meaning over presentation — https://html.com/semantic-markup/
12. **Web forms** – form controls and validation — https://html.com/forms/
13. **Embedding modern media** – audio/video — https://html.com/media/
14. **Character codes / symbols** – HTML entities — https://html.com/character-codes/
15. **Reference** – tag & attribute reference, cheat sheet — https://html.com/tags/
