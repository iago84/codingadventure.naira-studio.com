PER-VOLUME WORKFLOW
1. Read the tutorial pages in order (web fetch / scraping with polite delays, respect robots.txt and each site's terms).
2. Write ORIGINAL notes in English, in your own words. Do NOT copy text verbatim and do NOT reproduce full pages, images or code blocks wholesale from the sources. Short snippets are fine only when needed, with attribution. Many of these sites are copyrighted; the PDFs are personal study material.

- Final test: 20–30 mixed questions covering the whole volume, with answers
